### Textbooks

1. Principles of CMOS VLSI Design: A Systems Perspective, Neil H.E. Weste, David Harris, Ayan Banerjee, Pearson Education. (See Chapter on Memory Design and Sequential Circuits)
2. The VLSI Handbook, Second Edition (Electrical Engineering Handbook) by Boca Raton: CRC. ISBN 0-8493-4199-X. (See Memory Design and Latch/Flip-Flop sections)
3. CMOS: Circuit Design, Layout, and Simulation, Third Edition by R. Jacob Baker, Wiley-IEEE. (Includes chapters on latches, flip-flops, and memory cells)
4. The Spice Book, Andrei Vladimirescu, John Wiley & Sons, Inc.
5. Electronics (Fundamentals and Applications), D Chattopadhyay
6. System Integration: From Transistor Design to Large Scale Integration, Kurt Hoffman
7. A Guide to Circuit Simulation and Analysis Using PSpice, Paul Tuinenga, 3rd Edition, Prentice-Hall.
